## Hard limits (apply to every marketing task)

1. **Users stay anonymous, always.** No sign-up, email, phone, name or ad ID is collected or asked for in any campaign, form, waitlist or survey.
2. **No health or outcome claims.** Never say or imply the app helps people quit, reduces cravings, improves health, or works for X% of users. Describe what the app *is* and *does* (features), not what it will *do to the user*.
3. **US-centric advice must be checked** against EU rules and App Store rules before use (see "Rules to check against").

## Rules to check against (before using US-centric advice)
These are areas to check, not legal advice. Each needs verifying against the current source.
- **App Store Review Guidelines** — health/medical claims and accuracy (section 1.4), misleading metadata and screenshots (2.3), privacy and data (5.1). Subtitle and screenshots may not carry unverifiable claims.
- **EU Digital Services Act (DSA) trader status on the App Store** — if the developer is a trader, Apple shows their name, address, phone and email publicly on EU listings. [ASSUMPTION — verify current Apple requirement and how it applies to a sole developer.]
- **GDPR and ePrivacy** — no tracking, ad IDs or analytics cookies without consent; anonymity rule is stricter anyway. US tactics like retargeting pixels, email capture and lookalike audiences are out.
- **EU Medical Device Regulation (MDR 2017/745)** — software whose stated purpose is to treat or prevent a condition (e.g. nicotine dependence) can count as a medical device. Marketing wording helps define that purpose. [ASSUMPTION — needs checking; it's a key reason for hard limit 2.]
- **EU Unfair Commercial Practices Directive** — no misleading claims, fake urgency, or fake reviews. Hits US tactics like countdown timers and "only today" pricing.
- **Rules on e-cigarette advertising (Tobacco Products Directive and Belgian law)** — check whether ads or visuals showing vape products are restricted, even in a quit context. [ASSUMPTION]
- **Ad platforms' own health/addiction policies** (Apple Search Ads, Meta, TikTok, Google) — check before any paid campaign.
